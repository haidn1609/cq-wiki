Total War Basic Mechanics
9
PassiveYou only have 20 seconds to kill each phase. If you cannot kill within 20 seconds, you lose.
10
For this battle, all hero will be forced into auto delegation, and their champion bond bonus will be removed.
11
For this battle, buff can not be shared between teams. On the other hand, debuff will benefit all four teams.
12
Yellow Emperor has 8 phases. After each death, boss will revive and receive stackable buffs and apply stackable debuffs onto your heroes based on difficulty.
13
Champion bond bonuses are not applicable in this mode.
14
Mechanics upon Revive
15
All DifficultyUpon revive, immediately force cleanse all debuffs and marks from self, and remove all buffs from your heroes.
Force cleanse debuffs and marks can remove effects that are otherwise unremovable.
Additionally, Yellow Emperor will immediately terminate all ongoing skills and reset attack pattern.
16
HardBoss Buffs: 10% Damage Done / 10% Apen / 10% Rpen / 10% DR / 10% CR.
Team Debuffs: Reduce ally heal received by 12% / Drain 100 SP / Shred 20% Apen & Rpen.
17
NightmareBoss Buffs: 15% Damage Done / 10% Apen / 10% Rpen / 10% DR / 10% CR.
Team Debuffs: Reduce ally heal received by 12% / Drain 100 SP / Shred 80% Apen & Rpen.
18
InfernoBoss Buffs: 20% Damage Done / 10% Apen / 10% Rpen / 10% DR / 10% CR / 20% CC / 40% CD.
Team Debuffs: Reduce ally heal received by 12% / Drain 100 SP / Shred 95% Apen & Rpen.

Support
20
Support HeroYou can select up to 16 hero from the 3 listed factions as support.
The total max HP of the heroes determines the support effectiveness.
Factionless hero, namely collab hero, can also be selected. However, their total HP will be cut in half.
21
The support gauge is charged by dealing damage to the boss. The amount of dealt damage required to fully charge the gauge depends on the selected difficulty.
The requirement is: 200,000,000 [Hard] / 400,000,000 [Nightmare] / 600,000,000 [Inferno].
22
The formula for support damage is given as:
Damage = Sum of Support HP * Champion Count * (0.75 + 0.25 * Number of Unlocked Bond Skills + 0.05 * Total Bond Levels) + 1,000,000.
This damage will always deal a fixed amount, ignoring enemy DR or Damage Taken increase debuff.

Regular Attacks
24
Basic AttacksYellow Emperor will randomly use one of the following regular attacks [Palm Sweep] / [Palm Barrage].
25
Palm SweepThe boss does a palm sweep from right to left, dealing [5250] neutral damage and significantly knocking right side hero towards boss, and left side hero away from boss.
26
Palm BarrageThe boss unleashes a series of palm strikes, dealing [1050 * 10] neutral damage to all heroes.
27
Special AttacksAgainst Left Side HeroAgainst Right Side Hero
28
After every regular attack, Yellow Emperor will randomly use one of the following special attacks [Ying Yang Barrage / Tides of the Far Shore].
All Special Attacks ignores tanking.
29
Ying Yang BarrageYellow Emperor unleashes a barrage of black energy orbs, dealing [3200 * 12] physical damage, stun hit heroes for 1 second and knocking them away from the boss.
In Nightmare / Inferno, also reduce Arm by 5000 [Nightmare] / 10000 [Inferno] for 5s.		Yellow Emperor unleashes a barrage of white energy orbs, dealing [3200 * 12] magical damage, dispels hit heroes and knocking them away from the boss.
In Nightmare / Inferno, also reduce Res by 5000 [Nightmare] / 10000 [Inferno] for 5s.
30
Tides of the Far ShoreYellow Emperor unleashes a Tide of Darkness, dealing [1750 * 30] physical damage, stun hit heroes for 1 second and knocking them towards the boss.
In Nightmare / Inferno, heroes cannot be healed for 5 seconds.		Yellow Emperor unleashes a Tide of Light, dealing [1750 * 30] magical damage, dispel all hit heroes and knocking them towards the boss.
In Nightmare / Inferno, reduce hit heroes SP to 0 and prevent SP for 5 seconds.
31
Palm LaserAfter 2 sets of basic attack and special attack, Yellow Emperor shoots lasers from both hands, sweeping across the battlefield dealing [5250] neutral damage.
When cast, this attack reduces hero Apen & Rpen by 30% for 3s [Hard] / 70% for 4s [Nightmare] / 99% for 10s [Inferno].
Yellow Emperor also will immediately use this attack at the beginning of an Inferno run.
32
HellfireWhenever Yellow Emperor revives, unleash Hellfire, dealing [3000] neutral damage every second. This attack ignores tanking.
In Inferno, all heroes additionally lose 1 SP every 0.1 seconds for the duration of the skill.
33
Dispel & CleanseWhen the timer reaches 14 and 6, Yellow Emperor will dispel all heroes and apply buff block for 1s. Simultaneously, cleanse itself and gain debuff immunity for 1s.

Beware of Where You Put Your HeroThe boss has different form of disruption depending on what side your hero is on. Organize your hero to minimize it.
42
Block GenerationYou don't have access to champion nor goddess here. So you need all the blocks you can get.
43
Damage Taken DebuffWhile buff isn't shared, debuff is. So make sure to bring some hero that has a debuff that increase damage taken.
44
Use Jack for Early GameJack is a very powerful hero to use here, but she only works for Hard and Nightmare, and become completely useless in Inferno.
45
Atk Debuff is UselessBoss doesn't use his attack to deal damage. So don't expect to be saved from inferno by debuffing the boss's atk.
46
Attack Speed DebuffWith some attack debuff, boss attack pattern can be changed from Auto => SP => Auto => SP => Laser into Auto => SP => Laser. Which means Boss DPS can be heavily reduced by applying attack speed debuff. Which means you should bring Himiko, or Basquiat if you don't have her. Note that Himiko will reduce Inferno Boss DPS by up to 75%. Tho he will still hit almost twice as hard as Nighmare.

You Need Accessories
So Spend More Money		You just need accessories. Without the drop from Total War, you just won't be able to make it far in this content. Accessories also cost a lot of money. On average, 4 star accessory costs 6.5m, 5 star 15m, 6 star 52m gold to fully upgrade on average. Not to mention all the limited hero you will need for this.
