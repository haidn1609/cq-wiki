Game Currency
GoldThe basic resource in the game. Can be used to upgrade weapons, feed bread and berries, transfer skills, buy things from popo, inlay or take out sigils, and so on.

Gold may be obtained primarily through friend honor exchanges, quests, fishing, story and challenges stages. It can also be bought in large quantities in the fishing shop, which will always be your main source of gold income. Note that farming gold outside Fishing is not recommended as they are extremely inefficient.

Side note, selling high rarity useless identified sigil in bulk can also be a decent gold income. While there is the option of saving up for a box, what you get is completely RNG, and the box itself already cost a fortune, not to mention the money you lost by not selling those sigil.
6
MeatMeat should be self explanatory. It is a resource required to play story mode, challenge mode, and raid stages. Meat can be obtained from many sources, including sending honor to friends, quests, colosseum, and may be bought in large quantities from the fishing shop. If you do not have enough meat, the 30-gem meat package from the shop is not a bad deal either.

Although meat seems like an abundant resource at first, when it comes to mid to late game farming, you would begin to realize that meat isn't as plentiful as it seems. You will need meat almost everywhere, and combined with the low drop rate of sigils in challenge and raid, you will certainly need as much of it as you can.

KeyThis should also be quite obvious. Keys are used to enter dungeons stages, and sometimes, certain event dungeon stages as well.

Keys can be obtained through daily and weekly quests. They can also be bought from travelling merchant Popo. Popo from episode 6 onwards may sell 10 keys in a single slot.

Hoarding key can be considered in the following situation:
1. You need resu rings => hoard key for fergus ring event
2. You need crystals => hoard key for weekened acceleration dungeon
3. You want to have a high ranking in collection event

Colosseum TicketColosseum tickets, as their name suggests, is used to enter colosseum. As colosseum is the only sustainable source of gems in game without paying, this resource can be very valuable when competing for higher percentage brackets.

Apart from the regenerating tickets and from daily quest, where both of which are limited, the only other source of colosseum tickets are from travelling merchant Popo. When you see the phrase "farming Popo", this is often the primary purpose.

Traveling Merchant PopoPopo is a chance encounter limited time shop which you can find when doing any scenario or challenge stages.

She has a 1/8 chance to appear every time you complete a scenario or challenge stage, but only Popo from Deep Sea and beyond can sell ticket, and Popo from Battlefield and beyond can sell more keys per slot. Later, popo will start selling pages, and challenge popo will sometimes sell you a chest from that challenge mode, at a very reasonable price.

Note that Popo found in Forest and Desert have 4 slots, Popo found in Deep Sea and Volcano have 6 slots, and Popo from Tundra and beyond have 8 slots.

The recommended farming spots for Popo is the highest stage with the lowest meat cost in Tundra (5-8), and Battlefield (6-3). The former is preferred when tickets is prioritized for its lower meat cost, and the latter is preferred when you want more keys.

GemThe semi-premium currency in game. Gems are primarily used to pull for heroes and to expand inventory slots. Do not use gems to reset hero promotions.

Beginners can quickly amass a large amount of it through max levelling heroes they have not maxed before, as well as through various story quests. After the beginner gem pool, the primary source of gem income is colosseum.

HonorHonor is used primarily for promoting heroes, and is also a necessary resource for upgrading skills as well. Honor is obtained primarily from the honor dungeon. Excess honor can be be used to buy hero home decorations.

You can use BP to guarantee great success on weapon upgrades and skill transfers, however it is considered a poor usage of the limited currency. It is recommended to save up BPs hero selection tickets. The value of seasonal hero selection ticket is debatable.

Gem Fishing SetBy Gem Fishing Set, it refers to the Bamboo Rod and Seiren Doll / Rubber Ducky.

It is recommended for a player to first purchase the Seiren Doll from the fishing shop as soon as you can as it is unlocked at Fishing Rank 1. The earlier you buy it and use it, the faster it pays off. The items in the fishing shop are very lucrative. Auto fish overnight for passive fish coin income. It is suggested to use an emulator to avoid destroying your device's battery life.

It should be noted that the fish coin sets aren't completely outclassed when compared to gem set. From the (not very reliable) data gathered from the survey, gem sets are around 20-40% better than fish coin sets. However, it would not be very possible for you to amass 600k fish coins in a short time to immediately make use of the fish coin set, which may severely hinder your progression.

From a long term perspective, the Seiren Doll is definitely a worthy investment. It is up to you, the player, to determine whether you want to save the 99 gems in exchange for a much slower initial progression.

Note that there are zero differences, fish coin income wise, between the Rubber Ducky and Seiren Doll, as the terrain bonuses affects nothing but fish size. For fishing events, the difference is also nonexistent for it takes place in neutral water.

Two Dimensional PotentialTwo-Dimensional Potential rings are extremely valuable items obtained from certain events. They are fixed to 2 sub options and always has a CC main stat at a 6* Manacar ring equivalent.

What sets this ring apart from regular 6* CC rings is, you can roll LS on its sub options (6% per slot, 12% max), and because this is what makes this ring unique, you will always want to roll for this specific sub option.

There are generally two cases when this ring would see use.

1. When you need more than 8.1% LS (the maximum amount to which regular LS main stat rings can provide). Events such as Don't Mess with Fergus or Hungry Hungry Fergus typically have those requirements.

2. When you need both CC and LS. FoRS Stage 3 Rael is one such example, where she has 60% CR while demanding more than 7% LS (else you die to reflect). Your damage dealers in ordeal or arena who can use the additional damage alongside survivability also fits into this criteria.

3. In PVE, as decribed later, when, due to either their own mechanics or to party composition, damage dealers gain no benefit from sub slots.

Rings and Relevant Contents#REF!AboutAs there are 3 primary relevant contents in the game, ring recommendations can be split into the 3 sections, Colosseum, Arena, and PvE.
36
ColosseumSubslots matter more than main options. This is because the "perfect" ring in colosseum is so incredibly rare that it does not justify throwing away one that isn't of the correct main option.

You cannot deal damage if you are dead. Therefore, to maximize survivability. here are some references to help you choose the optimal ring.

1. Resu ring always take priority, optimally with CR subslots.

2. DR rings > CR rings when there is no external sources of DR buff.

3. CR rings > DR rings when there are external sources of DR buff (e.g. Caeneus' 40% DR self buff).

4. CR rings >> DR rings when your opponent has high eCD (effective crit damage, in other words, CD * CC).

5. Heroes that focuses on stacking Evasion should always use Eva ring above all else.

If your damage dealer does not have any issues surviving, then rolling the 2 other subslots into magical / physical damage increase depending on your damage type (ignore this if your DPS uses neutral damage) can provide better quality of life.

For primary options, LS on damage dealers have the most impact when it comes to survivability as it is essentially a second passive, then followed by offensive options (CC, CD). Armor and Resis main stats may benefit tanks, whereas HA main stat can benefit buffers and healers that scale off of it.
37
#REF!ArenaCOLOSSEUM ONLY RINGS ALSO WORK IN ARENA. If you didn't know, now you do.

The only difference that sets ring recommendations apart from arena to colosseum is that, resu rings does not work in arena. Therefore, the following points should be used as reference for arena from a survivability perspective:

1. CR rings > DR rings, unless the hero in question is not receiving any external DR buffs. Rare, but not impossible (e.g. resu Halla).

2. Heroes that focuses on stacking Evasion should always use Eva ring above all else.

However, arena isn't just about survivability. After all, assassinating certain threats before they murder you is also of utmost importance, seeing as these would have no counter otherwise.

This means that heroes that employ the "kill them before they kill you" strategy (often fast, bursty, tank ignore damage dealers) would prefer rings with physical / magical damage increase or Accuracy to ensure you can effectively carry out this strategy.
38
#REF!Some Explanation Regarding CRHow does CR work and why is it preferable over DR?

Let's begin with what CR does. The defensive capability that CR provides is directly correlated to the opponent's CD and CC. To put it mathematically, the effective damage reduction (eDR) that CR provides is calculated as:

eDR = 1 - (1 + CD * (CC - CR)) / (1 + CD * CC)

With the average base CD and CC in CQ being around 145% and 45% respectively, 10% CR would make for an average of 8.8% effective reduction in damage. Of course, this value becomes more effective the more CD your opponent has.

If you are interested in how CR behaves in an eDR perspective, play around with this graph, where y = eDR, x = CC, a = CD, and b = CR. Please don't touch anything aside from the slider bars.

Although DR functions in a similar way, where the more you have the more effective it is, it is very easy to cap out DR from various sources in arena without the use of DR rings, thereby the bonuses provided will go to waste.

As arena requires every single hero to be flexible, the minor advantages that DR has in cases where DR is not capped is not worth sacrificing the versatility of using CR rings. Oh yeah and in colo, CR can appear alongside resu, so that's that.

Beginners & General Farming Recommendations#REF!Beginners PriorityAfter all that information, as a beginner, what kind of rings should you hunt for?

It is generally a good idea to prioritize obtaining a ring with some accuracy subslots to reach the challenge accuracy requirement. Math out how much you need yourself depending on what DPSes you are running.

Otherwise, beginners should prioritize hunting for resu rings as a long term objective. However, if you obtain a ring with 2 Apen / Rpen subslots or with 2 accuracy subslots, it is a good idea to reroll the slot that is not the same as the previous two to complete a ring with 3 penetration / accuracy subslots.

Once you have reached end game, these will be of incredible help in challenge and arena, as well as in events such as ordeal and Hasla War. This advice is also applicable to other ring options, where it is recommended to keep any rings you get with 3 of the same slot (or any ring with combinations of Apen / Rpen and Accuracy) as they may be of use in the future.

5* penetration rings are as good as useless end game wise, seeing as not having the maximum upgrades can immensely affect your damage output due to Armor and Resistance's hyperbolic growth; it should be rather clear if you paid attention to CQ Maths 101.

Speaking of HP... Manacar HP options are in the triple digits at max, whereas LoPF HP% options only work on training stats (no inheritance, no berry, no sigils). That sounds totally useful, am I right?

#REF!FarmingThe intended way to obtain manacar rings is, well, by farming manacar. However, this is largely inefficient as your meat could be spent somewhere more useful. Still, for beginners, it is recommended to grind a couple rounds of it to obtain usable rings to supplement your main challenge carry.

If you are patient enough, you can grind LoPF indefinitely as long as you do not clear the stage. The optimal way to do this is via bringing an inherited hero along with 2 random low star heroes, with Ibelin as your champion (not necessary, but can significantly speed up the grind).

Otherwise, the most efficient way to grind rings is to wait for Sneaky Sneaky Fergus Dungeon (or as we call it, ring dungeon) which returns every 2-3 months. From there, you can obtain a large amount of ring boxes and would provide you with a better chance of obtaining something useful.

Inlay and SBW
UpgradeRefer to the Definitely Original Tier List for recommended SBW options for every hero.

Upgrading weapons costs a LOT of gold when you are hunting for the desired option, and is generally your main expenditure of gold. You can upgrade your SBWs at any star level as the upgrade cost and the pool of options are the same regardless.

Save your gold or fish coins until Hammer Time, where the increased great chance can save you quite a bit of resources; your personal sanity included.
64
InlayingTo prior to inlaying sigils, you must use another SBW to punch a hole in the SBW you want to slot a sigil in. It does not have to be an identical SBW. Afterwards, you can put a sigil in the SBW. When you inlay a SBW that already has a slot, it does not destroy the sigil present on the SBW.

However, it is significantly harder to acquire double inlays (2 sigil slots). The second slot can only be unlocked by supplying extra materials, unlocking at 100 points.

4* SBW gives 5 points
5* SBW givess 10 points
6* SBW gives 20 points
A single fergus point gives 10 points

Resource wise, given how abundant old weapons are, supplying 20 4* SBWs is the cheapest way to obtain a second sigil slot. In comparison, using 5 6* SBWs will cost 50% more crystals, as well as 75% more iron and gold.

Fergus Points can also be used to achieve great upgrades, as well as to reset ring conversion limit. However, only do so if you have a lot of excess FPs, as these are still rare resources.
